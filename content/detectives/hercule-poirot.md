# Hercule Poirot

Hercule Poirot is one of the most famous detectives in fiction, created by Agatha Christie.

Unlike detectives who rely heavily on physical investigation, Poirot often solves cases by studying people, reconstructing events, and understanding the psychology behind a crime.

He believes that the smallest detail can reveal the structure of an entire mystery.

## The Poirot Method

Poirot's investigations rely heavily on:

- Observation
- Psychological analysis
- Logical reasoning
- Questioning
- Contradiction detection
- Reconstruction of events
- Understanding human motives

Rather than simply asking what happened, Poirot asks why someone would behave in a particular way.

## His Greatest Strength

Poirot understands that a crime is ultimately a human event.

People have motives.

People make mistakes.

People contradict themselves.

And people often reveal more than they intend to.

His method demonstrates an important principle:

**Understanding the person can be as important as understanding the evidence.**

## The EyeQ Lesson

Poirot represents another central EyeQ idea:

**Details become powerful when you understand their relationships.**

Observation gives you the clues.

Reasoning connects them.

Deduction turns them into an explanation.
