# Patrick Jane

Patrick Jane is the central character of *The Mentalist*, known for solving crimes through observation, psychological insight, deception, and careful deduction.

He rarely behaves like a conventional detective.

Instead of relying primarily on forensic evidence or traditional investigative procedures, Jane studies people.

He watches what they say, how they behave, what they avoid, and how they react under pressure.

## The Jane Method

Jane's greatest weapon isn't simply intelligence.

It's **attention**.

He constantly looks for small inconsistencies between what people say and what their behavior reveals.

He uses:

- Observation
- Body language
- Psychological manipulation
- Misdirection
- Memory
- Pattern recognition
- Social engineering
- Deductive reasoning

## His Greatest Strength

Jane understands that people reveal information constantly, often without realizing it.

A hesitation.

A glance.

A change in tone.

A contradiction.

A strangely specific detail.

To Jane, these aren't meaningless behaviors.

They're clues.

## The EyeQ Lesson

Patrick Jane represents one of EyeQ's central ideas:

**People are evidence.**

Learning to observe people doesn't mean assuming you can read their minds.

It means learning to notice behavior, establish patterns, identify inconsistencies, and form hypotheses without confusing them for facts.
