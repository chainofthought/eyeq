# Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes is perhaps the most influential detective in fiction.

Created by Arthur Conan Doyle, Holmes is famous for solving seemingly impossible mysteries through observation, deduction, scientific reasoning, and careful examination of evidence.

He sees information where others see ordinary details.

## The Holmes Method

Holmes relies on:

- Observation
- Deductive reasoning
- Scientific analysis
- Forensic examination
- Pattern recognition
- Knowledge
- Hypothesis testing
- Careful questioning

His investigations often begin with something extremely small.

A mark on a shoe.

Ash from a cigarette.

A particular type of handwriting.

A missing button.

To Holmes, these details are evidence.

## His Greatest Strength

Holmes understands that observation is only useful when combined with reasoning.

Seeing a clue is one thing.

Understanding what that clue means is another.

His greatest skill is connecting small observations into a larger explanation.

## The EyeQ Lesson

Sherlock Holmes represents the foundation of the detective mindset:

**Notice what others overlook.**

But observation alone isn't enough.

A detective must distinguish between:

**What you observed.**

**What you inferred.**

**What you actually know.**

That distinction is at the heart of good reasoning.
