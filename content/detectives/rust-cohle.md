# Rust Cohle

Rust Cohle is a detective from *True Detective*, known for his intense intelligence, philosophical worldview, and unconventional approach to investigation.

He doesn't simply investigate what happened.

He tries to understand the deeper structure behind human behavior, violence, and motive.

Cohle is patient, obsessive, and extremely attentive to details that others dismiss.

## The Cohle Method

Cohle's investigative style combines:

- Observation
- Pattern recognition
- Psychological analysis
- Interrogation
- Logical reasoning
- Evidence reconstruction
- Relentless questioning

He is particularly interested in contradictions.

When someone's story doesn't fit the evidence, Cohle doesn't simply accept the explanation.

He keeps digging.

## His Greatest Strength

Cohle's greatest strength is his ability to question assumptions.

He doesn't automatically accept the obvious explanation.

Instead, he asks:

**What if the story everyone believes is wrong?**

That mindset allows him to notice connections between seemingly unrelated pieces of evidence.

## The EyeQ Lesson

Rust Cohle represents one of EyeQ's most important principles:

**Never confuse the first explanation with the truth.**

Good investigation requires curiosity.

Better investigation requires skepticism.

The strongest investigators are willing to question even their own conclusions.
